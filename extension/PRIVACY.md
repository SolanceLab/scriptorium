# Scriptorium Export: privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026.

Scriptorium Export collects no data. It has no server, no account, no analytics, no tracking and no remote code.

**What it does.** When you click Export on a claude.ai, chatgpt.com or grok.com conversation page, the extension reads that one open conversation from the page you are logged in to, shows you a preview, and, only when you click Save, writes it as a file on your computer through your browser's download dialog.

**What it transmits.** Nothing. The only network requests are the page's own requests to the site you are on, made to read the one conversation you opened, with your existing login. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.

**What it stores.** Nothing. It keeps no copy, no history and no settings; the file you save is yours.

**Permissions.** `activeTab` and `downloads` (to save the file you choose), and access to claude.ai, chatgpt.com and grok.com only, to read the open conversation and save the file.

**Limited Use.** The use of information received from these sites adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements: it is used only to provide the copy-to-file feature you asked for, is not transferred to anyone, is not used for advertising, and is not read by people.

Unofficial; not affiliated with OpenAI, Anthropic or xAI.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/SolanceLab (the Scriptorium repository).
